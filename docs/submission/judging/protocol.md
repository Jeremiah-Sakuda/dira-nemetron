# Three-round internal judging panel

Requested by the owner on October 18, 2026. These are independent AI review simulations, not official hackathon judgments or predictions of winning.

## Official basis

Rules rechecked October 18: [rules](https://nebiusglobalaihackathon.devpost.com/rules), [overview](https://nebiusglobalaihackathon.devpost.com/), [organizer judging explanation](https://nebiusglobalaihackathon.devpost.com/updates/46204-here-s-how-judging-works).

Stage One checks viable track fit and meaningful required technology use. Stage Two weighs Technological Implementation, Design, Potential Impact and Quality of the Idea equally. Submission compliance is tracked separately: working free access through December 23 (current rules rechecked October 30), public licensed source, English materials, public YouTube video under three minutes, track selection, provider feedback and a significant-update explanation for this existing project.

## Review method

Three independent reviewers each score all four criteria, emphasizing engineering, product design and audience/idea respectively. Each round preserves its source commit, deployment state, evidence and original scorecards. The implementation pass then addresses findings and records measured checks. The next round judges the revised product. Repeat exactly three judge→iteration cycles, with a final verification after iteration three.

The 0–10 scale is an internal calibration, not an official Devpost scoring scale: 0–3 absent/broken; 4–6 partial or weak evidence; 7–8 coherent and demonstrated; 9–10 compelling, clearly demonstrated execution. The panel mean weights every criterion equally. Potential Impact follows the official wording: a credible, specific problem and audience, and a demonstrated solution that addresses that problem. User studies, adoption metrics and time-saving measurements are not additional requirements or reasons to deduct points. Do not force scores to rise or hide residual blockers.

Reviewers must distinguish production, synthetic integrations, operator fixtures and separately measured sandbox checks. A passing test or source implementation is not live deployment proof. Credential-free fallback availability is distinct from live inference availability. Scores do not override Stage One or compliance gaps.
