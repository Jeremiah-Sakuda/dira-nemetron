One email moved my exam forward 48 hours. My agent noticed before I did.

It re-derived the week: rebooked my interview from recruiter-approved slots
(the first one 409'd — it observed, replanned, booked the next), delegated
deck QA to our designated backup, reclaimed two personal blocks, rebuilt my
study plan on Google Calendar, and verified every requested change with fresh
reads. Zero prompts. Global slack: −3.6h → +1.3h.

The trick isn't the model — Gemini only *interprets*. A deterministic solver
owns feasibility, a policy engine owns authority, a durable ledger + verifier
own reality. The demo is one command, no credentials: `make demo-replay`.
